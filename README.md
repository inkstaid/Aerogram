# Aerogram

![Aerogram](cover.jpg)

Paper-plane letters for people far apart.
Write a letter, watch it fold itself into a paper plane (or crumple into a ball), and throw it off your screen. It flies onto the other person's screen and lands on their Dock (or taskbar). They click it, and it unfolds back into your letter.

An *aerogram* is a letter that folds up to be its own envelope. This is that, for your desktop.

## Download

**[⬇ Download Aerogram for Mac](https://github.com/inkstaid/Aerogram/releases/latest/download/Aerogram.dmg)**: macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

**[⬇ Download Aerogram for Windows](https://github.com/inkstaid/Aerogram/releases/latest/download/Aerogram-Setup.exe)**: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

Mac and Windows people can write to each other.

## Install on Mac

1. Open `Aerogram.dmg` and drag **Aerogram** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Aerogram from Applications.
3. The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the app (it isn't from the App Store). Click **Done**, then go to
   **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Aerogram. You only do this once.

A ✈ appears in your menu bar. Click it and everything is in one panel.

## Install on Windows

1. Run `Aerogram-Setup.exe`.
2. The first time, Windows may say **Windows protected your PC** (the app isn't from the Store). Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for you only, with no admin password.
3. A ✈ appears in the system tray (bottom right; click the ^ if it's hidden). Click it and everything is in one panel.

> **Coming from Mailbox 0.1?** Aerogram is the new name of Mailbox. Install Aerogram and you stay signed in with the same people; you can delete the old Mailbox app.

## Add people

1. Click ✈ → type your name → **Move in**.
2. **+ Add someone** → **Get a code to give them**. It looks like `KQ7M-2XHP` and works once, for 48 hours.
3. They do the same on their Mac, then **+ Add someone** → paste your code → **Join**.

Add as many people as you like. Every letter goes to **one** person: there are no groups and no broadcasting.

## Send a letter

| Do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| ✈ → **Write** next to a name | A sheet of paper opens, addressed to them |
| Choose ✈ or the crumpled ball, then send | It folds into a plane, or crumples into a ball |
| Fling it off the screen | It flies (or bounces) onto their Dock and waits |
| They click it | It unfolds, or smooths out, into your letter |
| The panel | **Sent** shows *In the air · Landed · Unfolded ♥*; **Received** keeps the letters you've opened |

If they're offline, the letter waits and arrives the moment they're back. A green dot beside someone's name means Aerogram is open on their Mac right now.

## What's new in 0.2.1

- **Windows.** Aerogram now runs on Windows too, with the same planes, balls and panel.
- **Photos.** Tape up to 3 photos onto a letter, from your library or the camera. They're printed photos with tape, not a picture in a feed: you drag them where you like, they slip inside when the letter folds, and land on the page when it's opened.
- **Keep ★.** A star under the last line keeps a letter, even after Clear messages.
- **Reply, Save as picture.** Answer from the letter itself, or save it (with its photos) as a picture on the sky.
- **Change your name** (✎) and **remove a person** (✕, which deletes your letters with them).
- **Open at login** and a version number in the panel.
- **Updates install inside the app.** Click the update banner and Aerogram swaps itself and reopens.
- **Fixes.** No more stuck "JWT expired" screen after being away, and no blank Settings window (Mac).

Everyone on 0.2 can read the text of a letter with photos, but only 0.2.1 shows the photos. Install 0.2.1 once by hand; after that, updates install themselves.

## What was new in 0.2

- **Paper ball.** Crumple a letter and throw it. It bounces and rolls in, and keeps its wrinkles when opened.
- **Many people.** Add as many contacts as you like, each one-to-one. With several people writing to you, planes and balls carry a small name tag.
- **Online status.** A green dot shows who has Aerogram open.
- **One plain menu bar icon.** The plane turns solid when mail is waiting.
- **Update notice.** Aerogram checks here for new versions and shows a small orange ↑ when one is out.
- **Clear messages** tidies your lists without deleting anything.
- A new icon and a new installer window.

## Good to know

- Your account lives on this Mac. There's no email or password yet, so there's nothing to sign out of.
- Letters are stored on a server so they reach your person even when their Mac is off. Only the two of you can read them.
